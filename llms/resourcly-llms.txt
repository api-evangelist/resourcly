# Resourcly

> Resourcly is an AI-powered platform for deep inventory optimisation across manufacturing. It harmonises fragmented PLM and ERP parts data, finds duplicate and substitute parts via similarity and visual search, and turns a reconciled inventory core into working-capital and part-availability gains. The public REST API (Swagger 2.0, Firebase-JWT authenticated) covers documents, items, ERP inventory, BOMs, suppliers, storage, search and analytics.
